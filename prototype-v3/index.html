How product data works
This prototype uses your original catalog for product names, images, brands, reference prices, and product links. It is a curated snapshot—not a live feed. Current availability is unknown and prices may have changed. A production catalog should use retailer-authorized affiliate feeds or APIs and keep each source URL and last-checked time. If current price or stock cannot be confirmed, MatchBuy should say “check retailer” instead of guessing.
