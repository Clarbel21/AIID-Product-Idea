Demo guide

StyleHub × MatchBuy AI in six steps.

  1. Home. Five real pieces from each house — Urban Revivo, Zara, Forever 21, Max Mara, 73Hours, Nike, Adidas, JW PEI, Songmont, DeMellier and Swarovski — every card links to the product’s own page.
  2. Pick a piece. Click any card: the AI asks your occasion and your feeling — cute, elegant, sexy or minimal.
  3. The matching algorithm scores every compatible piece on mood, occasion, colour harmony and house — so cute, elegant and sexy each build a different look.
  4. The assembled board shows top + bottom + shoes + bag + jewelry as one picture. Shuffle ⟳ re-scores a slot.
  5. Buy Now opens the piece directly on the house’s real website — StyleHub never handles payment.
  6. Wishlist. Tap ♡ anywhere, review from the masthead, keep it on this device.
Matching is a transparent scoring algorithm · all data simulated · Esc closes any overlay
